# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Entiqon Common community is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, safe, and respectful environment for everyone.

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Our goal is to foster an open and collaborative community around high-quality Go libraries and developer tooling.

---

## Expected Behavior

Participants in this project are expected to:

- Be respectful, courteous, and considerate toward others.
- Provide constructive and professional feedback.
- Welcome differing opinions and viewpoints.
- Focus discussions on improving the project and its community.
- Communicate respectfully in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project interactions.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling, inflammatory comments, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or collaborative environment.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate these guidelines.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions on participation in the project.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainer.

- **Maintainer:** Isidro A. López G.
- **Official Repository:** https://github.com/entiqon/common

All reports will be handled respectfully and, where appropriate, confidentially.
